About Me

I’m Arda Mavi. I compose music with a cinematic soul, often moody, bohemian, and rich in emotional texture. My solo works lean toward film-inspired soundscapes, while my alternative, rock, and hard rock compositions reflect various emotions, sometimes aggressive, sometimes melancholic. I play Electric and Bass Guitar, and recently the Violin and Piano have joined my musical journey. Some of my works are available on music platforms.
