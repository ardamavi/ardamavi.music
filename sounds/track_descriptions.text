Track Descriptions


Decadence
The piece was written in 2025 and is 1 minute and 45 seconds long. It was inspired by the 2019 psychological thriller Saint Maud (directed by Rose Glass). The composer creates a theme that matches the film’s atmosphere and strengthens the sense of the main character’s psychological collapse. The piece written for oboe and two cellos. With its limited number of notes and minimalist writing, the composer approaches it as a soundtrack-style work.

The Spirit Tree
The piece was written in 2025 and is 50 seconds long. This composition was created for a short clip from the film Nymphomaniac (2013, directed by Lars von Trier). In the film, the main character believes that every human soul finds its echo in a tree. During a mountain journey, she discovers her own tree. The score features two instruments: violin and violoncello. The beginning conveys a sense of mountain-climbing adventure, then intensifies the feeling of encountering the tree and falling into deep reflection. Finally, it ends with the image of the character and the tree, leaving space for the audience’s own interpretation of the scene.

Dodecett
The piece was written in 2025 and is 2 minutes and 39 seconds long. The composition begins with a soft introduction and gradually builds emotional weight; it is a short but melodically rich work. The ensemble consists of flute, oboe, bassoon, trumpet, trombone, tuba, bass drum, two violins, viola, cello, and double bass.
